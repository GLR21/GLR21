# Gabriel Lange Ramos

I spend most of my time on the unglamorous half of software: the XML schema nobody wants to open, the legacy module everyone routes around, the two systems that were never meant to talk to each other.

Full Stack Developer, backend-leaning. Systems Analyst at [Univates](https://www.univates.br). Based in southern Brazil, working in English, open to remote.

### The thing I'm known for

Brazilian universities can't issue a diploma anymore unless it's a digitally signed XML that validates against the Ministry of Education's schema. When that became mandatory, my institution's project had been stalled for three years and had three months left.

I got handed it in my first months on the job, when nobody on the team, in the registrar's office, or anywhere else knew how it worked. I learned the XSD, the signature chain, the SOAP and REST integrations and the document management system while building it, validated every fragment against the official schema, and shipped it. Years later I'm still the technical reference for it.

That project is basically my whole engineering philosophy: read the spec, verify against the source of truth, don't trust output you haven't checked.
